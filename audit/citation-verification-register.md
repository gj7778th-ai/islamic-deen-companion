## Verification protocol

1. Locate the exact original passage in a reputable edition.
2. Compare Arabic wording and translations without silently normalising disputed words.
3. Record source identifiers and edition-specific numbering.
4. For hadith, distinguish collection attribution, grading and disagreement about grading.
5. Separate revealed text, report, scholarly inference and modern editorial explanation.
6. Mark disputed interpretations as attributed, not as consensus.
