# Additional source integration — 2026-10-07

The following uploaded sources were reviewed for useful educational material and integrated into the app as additional topic/plate content rather than copied wholesale:

1. **Complete_Namaz-e-Muhammadi_-_Urdu.pdf**
   - Integrated into **Salah Evidence / نماز کے دلائل**.
   - Useful material: prayer sequencing, raf‘ al-yadayn evidence, Amin after al-Fatihah, ruku‘/sujud positioning, and hadith-reference notes.
   - The app distinguishes evidence from later fiqh conclusions and does not present every detail as undisputed.

2. **HISN-ul-MUSLIM_urdu.pdf**
   - Integrated into **Duas — Extended / دعائیں — تفصیلی**.
   - Useful material: morning/evening adhkar, sleep/waking, toilet, food/drink, home/mosque, travel, illness, grief, death/burial, family and protection supplications.
   - Exact wording/counts should remain tied to the original source or authenticated hadith reference.

3. **Andha_Dhund_Pairvi_Ka_Anjam_-_Urdu.pdf**
   - Integrated into **Fiqh Guide / فقہی رہنما**.
   - Useful educational principle: respect and learn from qualified scholars without treating a scholar or inherited group identity as infallible revelation.
   - Polemical attacks and sectarian labels from the source are not reproduced as uncontested facts.

## Typography requirement

All content plates use one consistent language-specific typography system:
- **English:** Calibri, LTR/left aligned, dark green.
- **Urdu:** Noto Nastaliq Urdu, RTL/right aligned, maroon.
- **Arabic:** Noto Naskh Arabic, RTL/right aligned, green.
- Headings remain consistently styled; English and Urdu text are not mixed into one directional text block.

## Evidence handling

Qur’an and authenticated hadith remain the primary evidence standard. Source-derived commentary is explicitly labelled and is not silently presented as revelation.
