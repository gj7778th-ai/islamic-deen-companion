# Additional Information Incorporated from the Three Newly Supplied Sources

The local app build has been expanded with source-based study plates derived from:

1. **Andha Dhund Pairvi Ka Anjam — Urdu** (4 scanned pages)
2. **Complete Namaz-e-Muhammadi — Urdu** (4 scanned pages)
3. **Hisn-ul-Muslim — Urdu** (177-page scanned/printed source)

## App additions

### Salah Evidence & Practice
Added bilingual study notes covering:
- prayer procedure as presented through hadith evidence;
- rafʿ al-yadayn and the hadith references displayed in the source;
- al-Fatihah, ruku, sujud, sitting/tashahhud and post-prayer remembrance;
- explicit distinction between source-derived evidence and recognised fiqh differences.

### Dhikr & Duas
Added bilingual notes from Hisn-ul-Muslim on:
- keeping the tongue moist with remembrance of Allah;
- the virtue of reciting the Qur'an, including the one-letter/ten-reward narration;
- keeping Arabic, Urdu and English visually separated with source references.

### Following Evidence / Taqlid
Added a neutralised study treatment of the Andha Dhund Pairvi source:
- its discussion of following evidence rather than merely group identity;
- the Qur'anic references used in that source;
- a balanced editorial distinction between legitimate qualified scholarship/fiqh disagreement and unsupported or blind claims;
- cross-linking with the existing Tawheed, Shirk and Dua material rather than duplicating it.

## Typography rule

All newly added information is placed in the same style of bilingual content plate:
- **English:** Calibri, left-to-right, blue/dark-blue text.
- **Urdu:** Noto Nastaliq Urdu, right-to-left, maroon text.
- **Arabic:** Noto Naskh Arabic, right-to-left, green text.
- Headings: consistent Calibri bold/dark green.
- Each English/Urdu pair is kept in the same plate and is not mixed directionally.

## Source handling

These additions are source-derived study notes, not a silent merger of every claim in the books. The original source titles remain identifiable, and the app should retain the distinction between:
- Qur'an/hadith evidence;
- the wording or interpretation of a supplied source;
- recognised fiqh differences;
- editorial explanation added for clarity.
